# Wasteland HD art packs

HD art for **Wasteland Reborn HD** (the Fallout 2 phone port,
[wasteland-reborn-installer](https://github.com/Linutesto/wasteland-reborn-installer)):
Fallout 2's art redrawn at twice the resolution by Qwen-Image-Edit 2511 on a
home RTX 4090, with the
[Wasteland HD tools](https://github.com/Linutesto/wasteland-hd-tools).

The packs are attached to this repository's releases; `packs.json` lists
them with their sizes and checksums, and the Wasteland HD app's updater
reads it.

| Pack | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| `arroyo-hd.zip` | Arroyo village: its floor and roof tiles, walls, scenery and items on the ground |

## These packs need your own Fallout 2

A pack contains only redrawn art, no game files: it does nothing without a
legally bought Fallout 2 (Steam, GOG or Epic) installed through Wasteland
Reborn. The packs are free and always will be.
